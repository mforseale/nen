## Исходный промпт

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: isolated hero illustration for Nen, a solemn gothic self-discipline website. Primary request: one large anatomically detailed human heart in deep oxblood and dark crimson, tightly bound with several fine ivory and silver metallic chains, on a perfectly pure black background. Visual reference is the user's attached artwork: red anatomical heart, white chains, black space, delicate red contours. Translate into a highly refined dark hand-engraved anatomical illustration, etched muscle fibers, branching vessels, subtle red rim light and tactile metallic chain links. Composition: centered upright heart occupying 80% of a square canvas, aorta and vessels clearly shaped, substantial black negative space around silhouette, chains wrap organically diagonally around heart and extend subtly sideways. The heart is the focal point. Keep the upper half of the heart visible for separately composited interactive daggers. NO daggers, NO knives, NO swords, NO hands, NO text, NO UI, NO frame, NO symbols floating around. No neon, no purple, no gradients in backdrop, no cartoon heart icon, no gore or blood splashes. Dramatic yet quiet, serious editorial digital artwork, mostly black and deep red with pale silver chain highlights.
